Privacy Policy

paintBrush (Chrome extension) · Last updated: October 26, 2026 (added picture upload)

The short version
  • We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information.
  • The extension works completely offline. It never sends anything over the internet.
  • Your child's colored pages are saved only on your own computer, inside the browser.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party code.

Who this policy covers

This policy explains how the paintBrush Chrome extension (“the extension”, “we”) handles information. The extension is a coloring book designed for children ages 2 to 9 and the grown-ups who look after them.

Information we collect

None. The extension does not ask for, collect, or transmit names, email addresses, ages, locations, photos, voice, device identifiers, browsing history, or any other personal information. It has no sign-in and no way to contact other people.

What is saved on your computer

To remember a child's work between visits, the extension uses Chrome's built-in local storage (chrome.storage.local). This data stays on your computer and is never uploaded by the extension.

WhatWhy
Colors and stickers placed on each pageSo pictures look the same when you come back
Recent undo steps for each pageSo “Undo” still works after closing the tab
Which pages are finished, and the last page opened in each age groupFor gold stars, “My finished pages”, and picking up where you left off
Settings: sound on/off, parent lock on/off, color-by-number on/offTo keep your choices
Pictures you upload in “Color your own picture” (converted to black-and-white line art) and the colors added to themSo you can keep coloring them later. Delete any picture with its ✕ button

The age group is chosen on screen each time and is only used to show the right pages. It is not stored as information about your child.

Pictures you upload

“Color your own picture” lets you choose an image file from your computer. The extension converts it into line art inside your browser; the image is never sent anywhere. Only the converted line art is kept, in the extension's local storage, until you delete it. Please only upload pictures you have the right to use.

Pictures you save or print

“Save picture” creates an image file and hands it to your browser's normal download feature, so it goes to your Downloads folder. “Print” uses your browser's normal print dialog. The extension does not keep or send copies.

Network access and permissions

The extension asks Chrome for one permission, storage, which is used only for the local saving described above. It makes no network requests, loads no remote code, fonts, or images, and contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party software development kits.

Children's privacy

Because the extension does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, there is no personal information for us to review, share, or delete. We designed it this way on purpose, in the spirit of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU/UK GDPR.

Deleting saved data

To erase everything the extension has saved, remove the extension from Chrome (chrome://extensions → paintBrush → Remove). To clear a single page, open it and use “Erase page”.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be included with the extension and the date at the top will change. We will not start collecting personal information without updating this policy first.
